Method

Composite properties are calculated server-side using Classical Laminate Theory (CLT) off-axis transformation, rule of mixtures for on-axis modulus, Halpin–Tsai for transverse properties, Nicolais–Narkis for particulate stress concentration, Rosen microbuckling for compressive limits, and Schapery bounds for CTE.

Scope

100+ constituents spanning aerospace carbon fibers, high-temperature superalloys, advanced technical ceramics, natural fibers, structural foams, and engineering polymers. Prices are shown in Indian Rupees at ~₹96/USD.
